# Video Editor

</div>

A **modern GUI tool** for **editing, converting and combining videos and audio**, from quick everyday jobs to a full multi-track timeline, with precision, speed, and safety.

> _No command line | No risk | Full control_

---

## Features

- **Combine Video & Audio**: multi-track timeline for videos, pictures and audio, with keyframes, freeze frames and frame-accurate playback
- **Cut Video**: trim and join segments on a visual timeline
- **Hardcode Subtitles**: burn SRT, SUB, ASS, SSA, SBV or VTT subtitles permanently into a video
- **Extract & Convert Audio**: to MP3, WAV, AAC or FLAC, with optional trim
- **Speed Change**: from 0.25× to 4×, with or without keeping the original pitch
- **Rotate / Flip**: 90°, 180° or 270°, horizontally or vertically
- **Audio Amplifier**: raise the volume without distortion and reduce noise
- **Video Compression**: shrink a video to an exact target file size
- **Add Watermark**: image watermark with size, position and opacity
- **Video Converter**: convert between video formats with precise quality control
- **Multi-Feature Render**: watermark, subtitles, rotation, speed and conversion in ONE render
- **Templates**: save and reuse your subtitle styles, watermarks and conversion settings
- **Fast**: all CPU cores, GPU decoding and hardware encoding (NVIDIA, Intel, AMD), and the window never freezes
- **Safe by design**: originals are never modified, and everything is processed locally on your computer
- **Clean dark-mode interface**, no technical knowledge required

## Supported Formats

| | Formats |
|---|---|
| **Video input** | MP4, M4V, MKV, AVI, MOV, WebM, TS, M2TS, MTS, FLV, WMV, MPEG, MPG, 3GP, MXF, VOB, OGV |
| **Audio input** | MP3, WAV, W64, AAC, FLAC, OGG, M4A, WMA, OPUS, AIFF, AC3, MKA |
| **Picture input** | PNG, JPEG, BMP, WebP, AVIF, HEIC, TIFF, TGA, GIF, JXL, PSD, EXR, DDS, ICO and more |
| **Subtitles** | SRT, SUB, ASS, SSA, SBV, VTT |
| **Video output** | MP4 (H.264 / H.265 / AV1), MKV, MOV, TS, AVI, WebM |
| **Audio output** | MP3, WAV, AAC, FLAC |

## How to Use

1. Download the latest version from the [Releases](https://github.com/avarwand/AVE/releases) page and unzip it (run the installer for the installer version)
2. **Choose a feature** from the main menu
3. **Import** your files by click or drag & drop
4. Choose your **settings** and the **output path**
5. Click **Start**, or **Export** in Combine Video & Audio
6. **Done!**

## System Requirements

**Windows 10 / Windows 11**, with **FFmpeg** (free). If FFmpeg is missing, AVE installs it for you with one click.
